How It Works

Like a read-a-thon, with a basketball.

You pledge an amount for every free throw your shooter makes out of 100. They practice for two weeks, shoot on World Diabetes Day, and you give based on how many go in.

01

Pick a Shooter

Back a board member, your own kid, or a camper you know. Every shooter has a personal pledge page.

02

Pledge Per Make

$1 a shot, 50 cents, $5 — your call. The most anyone can make is 100, so you always know your ceiling.

03

Watch Them Shoot

Makes get counted on November 14 and your pledge is charged for the final total. Prefer a flat gift? That works too.

Find a Camper

For Camper Families

Give your camper their own page.

Every Diabetes Legends camper can shoot. A parent or guardian sets up and runs the page, and your camper does the shooting.

  1. Create the page (5 minutes). Click below, sign up as the parent, and add your camper as the shooter. Use a first name and last initial, and add a photo if you'd like.
  2. Share the link. Text it to grandparents, coaches, teammates, and neighbors. Most pledges come from family and friends who already know your kid.
  3. Practice November 1–13. Post a clip if you're comfortable sharing it, and tag @diabeteslegends so we can cheer them on.
  4. Shoot 100 by November 14. Have an adult count the makes, then enter the total on the page by November 15. We'll handle the rest.
Create My Camper's Page

Parents own the account, the email address, and the payment receipts. Campers never need their own login. Treat shoot day like any practice: check glucose first and keep fast-acting carbs courtside.

Questions

The fine print.

When does my card get charged?

After November 14, once your shooter's makes are entered. Pledges are collected through our fundraising partner, 99Pledges, and you'll get an emailed receipt.

What's the most I could end up paying?

Your per-shot amount times 100. A $1 pledge can never go above $100, even for a perfect day.

Can I just give a set amount?

Yes. Every pledge page also takes a flat donation, and you can give it any time.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

The Diabetes Legends Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your gift is tax-deductible to the extent the law allows. Keep your emailed receipt.

Where does the money go?

Free youth basketball clinics for kids living with Type 1, led by pro athletes who live with it too. Since 2023 we've hosted 360 campers across three cities, and every camper who applies gets a scholarship.

Does my camper have to shoot on November 14?

No. Campers can take their 100 shots any day from November 1–14. A parent enters the total by November 15.

My camper isn't a big basketball player. Can they still do it?

Absolutely. Showing up is the point. Lower the hoop, move closer, and count every make. A kid who goes 20 for 100 still raises money for the next camper.

Who do I ask if something isn't working?

Use the contact form and pick "Other." We'll get back to you within two business days.
