   inversion most brands share with Rob

## Why this compounds (the real argument)
A single brand cannot know whether a 0.53 skip rate is good. An agency running
diagnostics across a dozen accounts can. Every diagnostic sold makes the next one
more valuable and harder for a competitor to replicate. The benchmark dataset is
the moat, not the report. That is the case for pricing Tier 1 aggressively —
even at cost — to acquire data density.

## Upside not counted in the $100k
The diagnostic changes the win rate on new-business pitches for the core retainer
business. That is the larger prize and it is not in the numbers above.
