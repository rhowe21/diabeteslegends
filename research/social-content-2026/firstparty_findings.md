- NOTE: IG does not report media_follows for reels — so reels cannot be
  measured for follow conversion. Carousels/images are the ONLY formats

  proof reels drive zero follows — but it means the only *provable*
  follow driver in the account is the format he barely uses.

## C. Hook quality is empirically load-bearing
Reels split at median skip rate (n=70):
- Better hooks (avg skip 0.406) → median reach 2,580
- Worse hooks (avg skip 0.642) → median reach   910
=> 2.8x reach difference attributable to first-3-seconds retention.
Account median skip rate 0.534 — i.e. over half of viewers leave in <3s.
Median avg watch time 8.9s.
