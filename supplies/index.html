Before you call for a replacement

When a set or sensor fails early, the manufacturer will usually replace it — but they ask a lot of questions. Have ready:

  • Your pump serial number (SN on the back of the pump)
  • The lot number from the supply's box or wrapper
  • When it failed and what day of wear it was on
  • Your blood sugar at the time (a screenshot helps)
  • Date of birth and the shipping address on your account

Log the failure here when it happens and use Copy report in your history — everything above will be in one place.
